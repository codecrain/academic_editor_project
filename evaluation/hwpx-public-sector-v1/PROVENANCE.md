# Attachment provenance

`attachments.json` is the machine-readable source of truth for attachment
path, media type, SHA-256, byte length, publisher, retrieval date, license, and
role.

## Official sources

- 대한민국 교육부 2025 주요업무 추진계획 page: editable briefing HWPX,
  encrypted/distribution work-plan HWPX, PDF, preview PNG, and embedded cover
  JPG. License metadata: 공공누리 제1유형 출처표시.
- 대한민국 국가보훈부 공개 통계 page: 23-sheet veterans workbook and
  26-sheet beneficiaries workbook.

The beneficiaries workbook intentionally remains unmodified. Its
`기본현황(경합형태별현황)!Z7:AA40` range contains `#REF!` values and is used as
an adversarial remote-column formula trap.

## Repository and generated fixtures

- Minimal and structured HWPX templates come from versioned repository samples.
- The approximately 10 MB legacy HWP comes from the vendored RHWP sample
  corpus and is reference-only.
- CSV, TXT, and DOCX procurement evidence are synthetic. They contain no real
  personal data.

The DOCX fixture is generated by `scripts/build-supporting-docx.mjs`. Workbook
verification is implemented by `scripts/verify-supporting-xlsx.mjs`.

## Reproducibility and licensing

Do not silently replace an attachment. A byte change must update
`attachments.json`, regenerate the corpus, and be reviewed as a new dataset
revision. Public-source reuse must retain publisher attribution and the license
record. Repository fixtures are for internal compatibility evaluation unless a
broader license is documented.
